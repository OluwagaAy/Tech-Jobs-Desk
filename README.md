## 🎯 How to read a job ad

1. **Tools are keywords.** If the ad says Nessus, Burp or SIEM, recruiters search for those exact words. Put them on your CV if you have used them.
2. **Certs are filters.** Security+, CEH, A+, CCNA and ITIL show up again and again. Note which ones keep appearing for your level.
3. **"Years of experience" is flexible.** Meet about 70% of the requirements? Apply anyway.

## 📝 CV checklist

- [ ] 1 to 2 pages max
- [ ] Skills, tools and certs at the top
- [ ] Exact tool names copied from the job ad
- [ ] A **Projects / Home Lab** section (TryHackMe rooms, a Kali lab, a network you built)
- [ ] Numbers, not duties: "resolved 30+ tickets a week" beats "provided IT support"
- [ ] Saved as PDF: Firstname-Lastname-CV.pdf
- [ ] For email applications: their exact subject line and a 3-line body
